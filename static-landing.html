Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about SeKondly

What is SeKondly? +
SeKondly is a medical case consultation platform that connects healthcare professionals worldwide. It allows doctors to share complex cases, get expert opinions, and collaborate on challenging diagnoses to improve patient outcomes.
Who can use SeKondly? +
SeKondly is designed for licensed medical professionals, including doctors, specialists, residents, and medical students. All users must complete a verification process to ensure the platform maintains high professional standards.
Is SeKondly free to use? +
Yes, SeKondly is free to use for all verified medical professionals. We believe in making medical knowledge sharing accessible to improve patient care worldwide.
How do I verify my medical credentials? +
After signing up, you'll need to provide your medical license number, institution affiliation, and other relevant credentials. Our verification team reviews all submissions to ensure platform security and professionalism.
How is patient privacy protected? +
Patient privacy is our top priority. All case discussions must be completely de-identified, with no personal information, names, or identifiable details. We use advanced encryption and follow strict HIPAA compliance guidelines.
When will the mobile app be available? +
The SeKondly mobile app is currently in development. We expect to release it on both iOS and Android platforms in the coming months. Sign up for updates to be notified when it becomes available.
